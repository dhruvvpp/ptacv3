Starting a business in India involves a complex web of registrations, regulatory filings, and structural decisions. A wrong choice at the outset can have lasting consequences. Pratibha Tiwari & Co. guides entrepreneurs and businesses through the entire set-up process — ensuring the right structure is chosen, all registrations are obtained, and compliant processes are in place from day one.

What We Deliver

The right foundation from day one.

The decisions made when setting up a business have consequences that last for years. Choosing the right structure affects how you raise capital, how profits are distributed, and how you eventually exit. We help founders and business owners make these decisions with full information and professional guidance — then execute the setup process efficiently, correctly, and completely.
